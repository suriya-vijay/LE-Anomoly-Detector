    How it works

    The phone measures the saw's sound 2 times a second (70 numbers each time: loudness, pitch, brightness, rhythm and a 64-band frequency picture). Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

    GOOD / ODD / STOP compares each measurement with what good cuts on this saw sounded like. It learned only from good cuts, so it flags anything far from normal, including faults it has never seen.

    STOP shows only when the sound stays far from normal for 6 seconds with the motor running (yellow until then). This stops the motor winding down after switch-off from showing as STOP.

    Cut progress watches for the change in sound near the end of a cut: a little quieter and brighter. "Finishing" shows once the change holds for 15 seconds. It usually comes up to a minute before the blade is fully through.
